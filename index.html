🌳 Gabriella's Science Fair Lab: Save Our Trees!

Our big question: Does land with more trees produce less stormwater runoff and flooding than land without trees?

Inspired by new home construction in Charles County and The Lorax. Our model explores a possible effect of clearing trees; it does not measure flooding in Charles County.

🧪 Try it! Two-tray experiment

With an adult, prepare two same-sized trays with equal amounts of soil/sand. Add model trees to Tray A and few or none to Tray B. Use the same amount of water, poured from the same height and for the same amount of time. Measure collected runoff in mL. Repeat three times.

Safety: adult supervision for water and materials. Never experiment near actual floodwater or construction sites. Keep the trays over a waterproof surface.
